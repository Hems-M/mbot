The MBot family of educational robot platforms is designed at the University of Michigan. Since the MBot was invented in 2014, hundreds of MBots have been used to teach 8 courses across undergraduate and graduate levels.

Need help? Email um-mbothelp@umich.edu. Students: check with your course staff first, since your course may use its own setup.
