## What to use from this site

Your course website tells you what to do for each lab. This site has the general robot guides that every course shares:

- [Robot basics]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basics/): login, turning the robot on and off, battery care.
- [MBot Classic hardware]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/classic/hardware/): build steps, if you need to fix or rebuild your robot.
- [What is on the robot]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/guides/software-features/): every tool on the robot and when to use it.
- [Help]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/help/): fixes for the most common problems.

## For instructors and GSIs

The [MBot internal docs](https://mbot-internal-docs.github.io/) are for the development team, instructors and GSIs. They are not for students.
