## Before you start

- Each step starts with a **parts table**. Lay those parts out before you begin.
- Click any photo to see it larger.
- Some screws look alike. Check the length in the table (for example M2.5 x 6 mm or M2.5 x 8 mm).
- The camera mount and the OLED mount are both white 3D printed parts. They look similar but are different.
- This guide covers motors with **48 resolution** encoders. Not sure which you have? See [Which encoder do I have?]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/classic/software/calibrate/#which-encoder)
