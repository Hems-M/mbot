## 1. Mount the lidar

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Top plate | 1 |
| RPLidar A1 with USB board | 1 |
| M2 screws and nuts | 2 each |
| M2.5 x 8 mm screws | 4 |
| Micro USB cable | 1 |
| Zip tie | 1 |
{: .parts}

1. Push the small green lidar USB board through the opening in the top plate. Line it up with the holes.
2. Screw the USB board to the **underside** of the plate with two M2 screws and nuts.
3. Put the lidar on **top** of the plate, line up the holes, and fix it with four M2.5 x 8 mm screws.
4. Plug the micro USB cable into the USB board. Hold it to the plate with a zip tie.
{: .steps}

{% include gallery.html dir="/assets/images/hardware/omni/topPlate/" files="lidar1.jpg,lidar2.jpg,lidar3.jpg,lidar4.jpg" alt="Mounting the lidar" %}

## 2. Mount the OLED screen

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| OLED screen | 1 |
| OLED mount (3D printed) | 1 |
| M2 x 8 mm screws | 4 |
| M2.5 x 8 mm screws | 2 |
| M2.5 threaded inserts | 2 |
{: .parts}

1. Heat the two threaded inserts with a soldering iron and press them into the OLED mount.
2. Screw the screen to the mount with four M2 x 8 mm screws.
3. Screw the mount to the **right side** of the robot with two M2.5 x 8 mm screws.
{: .steps}

{% include gallery.html dir="/assets/images/hardware/classic/assembly/topPlate/" files="2-oled-mount1.jpg,2-oled-mount2.jpg,2-oled1.jpg" captions="Inserts|Inserts in place|Screen on the mount" %}

> **Done:** the top plate is ready. Next, the final wiring.
{: .tip}
