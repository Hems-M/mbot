## 1. Prepare the Raspberry Pi 4

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Raspberry Pi 4B (4 GB) | 1 |
| Heatsink case for the Pi | 1 |
| Raspberry Pi 5 MP camera with cable | 1 |
{: .parts}

1. **Thermal pads:** peel the plastic off both sides of the pads that come with the case. Stick them on the three chips on the Pi (or on the matching spots of the heatsink). The pads are already cut to shape.
2. **Camera cable into the Pi:** lift the black clip on the Pi camera port. Slide the cable in with the **metal contacts facing away from the clip**. Push the clip down. You should just see the contacts above the top of the port.
3. **Thread the cable** through the slot in the top of the heatsink case.
4. **Close the case:** screw the bottom of the case to the Pi with two of the included M2.5 screws, in two opposite corners. Longer screws go in the other two corners later, to hold the case on the plate.
5. **Cable into the camera:** open the black clip on the camera board. Insert the cable with the **metal contacts toward the camera side** (blue side to the back). Close the clip.
{: .steps}

## 2. Prepare the camera mount

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Camera mount (3D printed) | 1 |
| M2 x 8 mm screws | 2 |
{: .parts}

1. Screw the camera board to the mount with two M2 x 8 mm screws. They go straight into the plastic. Use two opposite corners.
2. Plug the USB-C to USB cable into the Pi as shown.
{: .steps}

{% include gallery.html dir="/assets/images/hardware/omni/middlePlate/" files="7.jpg,8.jpg" captions="Camera on the mount|USB-C cable in the Pi" size="md" %}

## 3. Build the battery holder

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Middle acrylic plate | 1 |
| Battery slot pieces | 5 |
| Zip ties | 4 |
{: .parts}

1. Zip tie **one** slot piece in the place marked with the **red** square.
2. Stack **four** slot pieces together in the place marked with the **blue** square.
{: .steps}

{% include gallery.html dir="/assets/images/hardware/omni/middlePlate/" files="3-battery-slot-1.png,3-battery-slot-2.png,3-battery-slot-3.png" alt="Building the battery holder" %}

## 4. Attach the Pi and camera to the plate

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| 4-40 x 1.5" aluminum standoffs | 4 |
| M2.5 x 20 mm screws | 2 |
| M2.5 x 8 mm screw | 1 |
| 4-40 x 1/2" screws | 4 |
{: .parts}

{% include gallery.html dir="/assets/images/hardware/omni/middlePlate/" files="9.jpg" captions="Ready to attach to the plate" size="md" %}

1. From under the plate, put two M2.5 x 20 mm screws up into the Pi case.
2. Screw the camera mount to the top of the plate with one M2.5 x 8 mm screw from underneath.
3. Screw the four aluminum standoffs to the top of the plate with the 4-40 screws.
{: .steps}

{% include gallery.html dir="/assets/images/hardware/omni/middlePlate/" files="10.jpg,11.jpg,12.jpg" captions="Pi on the plate|Camera mount on the plate|Standoffs" size="md" %}

> **Done:** the middle plate is ready. Next, build the top plate.
{: .tip}
